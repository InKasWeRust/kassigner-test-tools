A testing site, not a wallet

This page is a test tool for KasSigner owners. It is not a wallet and comes with no support.

Here you cannot send KAS, fund a card or claim a card.

A card made here is real: anyone can fund it from any wallet. To claim it, use the full tool or any SilverScript wallet.

The full tool is in its public repository, for you to build and run at your own risk.

Shown on every visit: this site keeps nothing in your browser.
